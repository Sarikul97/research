About Me

I am a Senior Research Fellow in the Department of Mathematics at IIT Kharagpur, specialising in Reliability Theory, Applied Probability, and Non-parametric Inference.

My research focuses on developing statistical models and non-parametric estimators for reliability of complex multicomponent coherent systems, stochastic ordering of system lifetimes under dependence structures modelled via Archimedean copulas, weighted distributions, and goodness-of-fit testing.

I am supervised by Dr. Nitin Gupta, Department of Mathematics, IIT Kharagpur.
